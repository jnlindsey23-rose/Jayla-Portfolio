Image Description

The image above displays the introductory graphic I created while editing a video for MetaMetrics featuring former NFL player and author Deon Butler. MetaMetrics conducted a podcast-style interview with Butler, and I was responsible for transforming the original hour-long recording into shorter videos for social media during October, which is Dyslexia Awareness Month. My responsibilities included identifying timestamps for relevant topics, selecting impactful quotes and segments that would engage viewers and promote the full interview, adjusting the video dimensions and margins for vertical mobile viewing, and designing introductory and closing graphics to create a polished, cohesive final product.

Project Purpose

The purpose of this project was to raise awareness about the importance of dyslexia screening, early identification, and support in schools and beyond. Deon Butler, a former NFL player, shared his experience of living with undiagnosed dyslexia and graduating high school with a fourth-grade reading level. His experiences with reading challenges heavily influenced his decision to leave professional football, leading him to become a motivational speaker and author who advocates for dyslexia awareness and shares his experience learning to read as an adult. My primary role in this project was to edit video footage using CapCut to create shorter, marketable videos for social media that highlighted key topics related to dyslexia, its impact, and the importance of awareness and support.
