About Me

Hi, I'm Jayla Lindsey. I am a graduate student at UNC Chapel Hill studying Educational Innovation, Technology, and Entrepreneurship. My background in the classroom and in education-related research sparked my interest in improving the way we approach teaching and learning. I am passionate about understanding the gaps within education and exploring how research, technology, and innovative tools can help address them. My interests include education product development, educational research, instructional practices, and the use of data to improve learning outcomes. I am currently completing an internship with MetaMetrics, where I am gaining experience in education products, market research, and data analysis.

Background

I graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Elementary Education from East Carolina University, where I gained experience both in the classroom and in education policy and program development. During my undergraduate studies, I completed two internships with the North Carolina Department of Public Instruction, working with the Center for Safer Schools and the Office of Teaching and Learning. Through these experiences, I learned more about how state education standards are developed, revised, and implemented. I also gained experience organizing and analyzing feedback data and collaborating with teams to plan and facilitate professional development events for educators across North Carolina. I currently hold a North Carolina K–6 teaching license.

Aspirations

I aspire to work in education product development and research, with a focus on creating reliable, effective tools that meet the needs of everyone within the education system, including teachers, students, administrators, and families. I am passionate about exploring how technology can be thoughtfully integrated into education to make learning more meaningful for students while making teaching more effective and manageable for educators. I believe technology has tremendous potential to transform education, but that potential depends on developing tools that are grounded in research and responsive to the people who use them. Working alongside educators to identify challenges, develop solutions, and continuously improve the public education system has always been a goal of mine—one that I intend to pursue throughout my career.

Goals

  • Design and improve innovative educational products by combining research, data, technology, and educator feedback to address real challenges in teaching and learning.
  • Bridge educational research, teaching practices, data, and innovation to develop practical solutions that support teachers and make learning more meaningful and effective for students.
  • Collaborate with educators, researchers, and education organizations to identify challenges within education and develop evidence-based solutions that improve teaching and learning across diverse educational settings.
