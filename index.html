About Me

Hi, I'm Jayla Lindsey. I am a graduate student at UNC Chapel Hill studying Educational Innovation, Technology, and Entrepreneurship. I earned my Bachelor of Science in Elementary Education from East Carolina University, where my experiences in the classroom and in education-related research sparked my interest in improving the way we approach teaching and learning.

I am passionate about understanding the gaps within education and exploring how research, technology, and innovative tools can help address them. My interests include education product development, educational research, instructional practices, and the use of data to improve learning outcomes.

I am currently completing an internship with MetaMetrics, where I am gaining experience in education products, market research, and data analysis.
